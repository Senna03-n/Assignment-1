Assessment Task 1 - Interaction Design Analysis

In this essay, I will analyse two UIs that I chose from the list which is Orb Farm and Liquid Entropy.

Information Design

The Orb Farm demonstrates ecological data in an engaging, interactive way. For instance, the visual elements are well-organized and easy to understand, featuring each interface button representing real-life environmental components. Furthermore, the CO2 meter brings in the environmental tracking aspect, which advances interactive data. Furthermore, the information design of the UI has followed the guidelines hierarchy which I found easy to access, resulting in a balance between usability and accessibility.

Liquid Entropy, on the other hand, takes a more abstract approach that emphasizes more on visual and auditory exploration rather than clear data presentation. For example, the control panel on the right allows users to freely manipulate the fractal patterns in continuous shifting to create a dynamic visual.

Overall, while Orb Farm focuses on clear, simplified data engagement, Liquid Entropy aims to generate an engaging, dynamic system that reacts creatively to user’s interaction.

Mapping

The Orb Farm’s mapping is apparent through the right panel, where users can interact with various environmental elements like Algae or Glass with simple button clicks. This mapping contributes to a relationship that is naturally comprehensible which the ecosystem changes in response to a button click.

In contrast to Orb Farm, Liquid Entropy uses more abstract mapping where users influence complex visual phenomena. For instance, the controls, such as zoom and vibrato amount, can be used to adjust abstract parameters to produce a visually captivating, dynamic fractal. In addition, the sounds correlate to visual alteration of the fractal patterns being produced. However, because there is no physical action like in Orb Farm but rather the manipulation of a developing visual system, I found that the result is challenging to map to real-world activities or thoughts.

To summarize, Orb Farm provides direct, user-friendly mapping between actions and outcomes while Liquid Entropy encourages users to interact with the system in more creative and imaginative ways by implying exploration-based mapping.

Characterisation

I believe that the pixelated art style has a significant impact on Orb Farm's characterisation. In more detail, the low-resolution, retro graphics evokes a comforting nostalgia as people frequently see the art style in their childhood, which creates a playful and light-hearted approach. Furthermore, the soft color palette and the tranquil background has reinforced the environmental care and growth theme. For example, the animation of buttons like Goldfish and Algae gives me the impression that I am a caretaker of a miniature world, which enhances the playful experience.

By contrast, Liquid Entropy’s orientation is more into a futuristic, surreal aesthetic. For example, the metallic background and flowing image are made up of constantly shifting patterns made with chrome material, which I believe have successfully emphasized the liquid entropy theme.

In general, Liquid Entropy's overall characterisation gives a sense of dynamic and mysterious, which is less about a connection to natural surroundings as in Orb Farm.

Feedback

As different from the purposes, Orb Farm and Liquid Entropy also differ from the user experiences. On the one hand, Orb Farm offers immediate feedback as the ecosystem visibly alters following users interact with buttons. Moreover, the visual feedback is based on real-life environments which make the result clear and comprehensible. For example, when users add fish, the fish will appear swimming. On the other hand, Liquid Entropy immerses users in a more abstract, experimental experience. When users adjust the settings, they can see visual changes and the sounds will also correspond to how they are adjusted. This creates a powerful sensory feedback loop that encourages exploration rather than direct cause and effect feedback as in Orb Farm.
